## Research Projects

### Brand Development Pathways for State Grid Public Welfare Projects
*Research Team Member (PI: Zhongsheng Wu)* · *Zhejiang University, School of Public Affairs* · *Nov. 2025 – Present*
- Conducted a multi-case study of 10 representative public-welfare projects selected from 24 initiatives under State Grid Zhejiang Electric Power.
- Examined the transition from project-based operations to systematic brand development using case analysis, in-depth interviews, and focus groups.
- Identified challenges (fragmented implementation, limited communication impact, weak sustainability) and proposed brand-oriented transformation pathways.

### Beijing "Youth Accompanying Silver Age" Mutual-Aid Elderly Care Project
*Research Team Member* · *Zhejiang University* · *Dec. 2025 – Present*
- Participated in a randomized controlled trial (RCT) across 40 communities in Beijing on an intergenerational mutual-aid elderly-care program ("Qing Ban Yin Ling").
- Administered and analyzed the Volunteer Functions Inventory (VFI) scale across treatment and control groups.
- Contributed to pre-test survey design, sampling, and power analysis.

### Digital Government and Megacity Governance
*Research Assistant, Digital Governance Lab (Advisor: Wenxuan Yu)* · *NSFC Major Project 24&ZD189* · *Sept. 2022 – Jun. 2025*
- Led a comparative study of megacity governance in London, Tokyo, Singapore, and New York; assessed the transferability of their models to a China-centered urban-governance theory.
- Synthesized 300+ articles and reports on agile, inclusive, digital, and crisis governance into the project's literature map.
- Contributed to the successful submission of the NSFC Major Project proposal (2024).

### Dongting Lake "10-Year Fishing Ban" Policy Research
*MPA Case Study Team, Core Member* · *Oct. 2023 – 2024*
- Surveyed 150 local fishermen and conducted 20 in-depth interviews with enforcement personnel.
- Built a policy-enforcement and emotional-governance framework for the decade-long ban; the case advanced to **Top 20% (Finalist), 8th China Graduate MPA Case Competition**.

### Cross-Boundary Collaboration and Social Co-Governance
*Research Assistant, Cross-Boundary Collaboration & Social Innovation Lab (Advisor: Haiying Lin, Hainan University)* · *Hainan NSF 720RC566; NSFC Major Project 20&ZD116* · *2021 – 2022*
- Conducted field research in He Le Town, Wanning; designed surveys and a data-analysis framework measuring co-construction / co-governance / sharing effectiveness.
- Co-drafted research and policy recommendations with Renmin, Fudan, and Tsinghua-Shenzhen collaborators.

## 研究项目

### 国网公益项目品牌化发展路径研究
*课题研究团队成员（PI：吴中盛）* · *浙江大学公共管理学院* · *2025年11月 – 至今*
- 从国网浙江电力的 24 项公益行动中筛选出 10 个代表性项目，开展多案例研究。
- 运用案例分析、深度访谈与焦点小组，探讨项目化运作向系统化品牌建设的转型。
- 识别实施碎片化、传播效能有限、可持续性薄弱等挑战，提出品牌化转型发展路径。
